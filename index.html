Xuewei Wu MD, PhD

Turning medical images into precision cancer care.

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Kather Lab at the Institute for Medical AI (IMAI) (opens in a new tab), Medical Oncology / NCT Heidelberg.

My research develops AI for image-guided precision oncology: imaging biomarkers that inform diagnosis and treatment, multimodal models that connect radiology, pathology, and molecular data, and clinical AI agents that support decision-making.

I enjoy working in multidisciplinary teams and am committed to advancing precision oncology through robust, clinically meaningful AI solutions.
