Xuewei WuMD, PhD

AI-driven medical imaging
for cancer diagnosis and therapy.

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Kather Lab at the Institute for Medical AI (IMAI), Medical Oncology / NCT Heidelberg.

With a background in radiology, I develop deep learning and radiomics models for molecular prediction, treatment response assessment, and prognosis across multiple cancer types.

My research interests span medical imaging, computational pathology, multimodal foundation models, and clinical decision agents. I enjoy working in multidisciplinary teams and am committed to advancing precision oncology through robust, clinically meaningful AI solutions.
