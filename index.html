Xuewei Wu MD, PhD

Bringing medical AI into real-world clinical care.

Hi, I’m Xuewei Wu (Chinese: 伍雪威). I currently work as a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Kather Lab at the Institute for Medical AI (IMAI) (opens in a new tab) and Medical Oncology / NCT Heidelberg, Heidelberg University Hospital (UKHD) (opens in a new tab).

Before moving into medical AI, I completed medical school and a three-year residency in radiology, and hold a Chinese medical license. I then earned my PhD at Jinan University.

I develop AI for precision oncology, spanning imaging biomarkers that inform diagnosis and treatment, multimodal models that connect radiology, pathology, and molecular data, and clinical AI agents that support decision-making.

I enjoy working in multidisciplinary teams and am committed to advancing precision oncology through robust, clinically meaningful AI solutions.
